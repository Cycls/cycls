# Notice

The Video tool's skills in this folder (video-direction, motion-design, promo-reel, explainer-video,
footage-video) were written for Cycls. Parts of them are adapted from the open projects below:
rewritten for Cycls's video format (Web Animations through `at()`, no animation library), its tool's
actions, its fonts and its Arabic-first audience, and changed throughout. No file here is a copy of
a file from these projects.
